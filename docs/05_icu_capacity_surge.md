# ICU Capacity Management and Surge Plan

**Policy number:** LGH-ICU-105 (synthetic)
**Owner:** Hospital Operations and Department of Critical Care Medicine
**Last reviewed:** April 2026

## 1. Purpose

Defines how ICU beds are managed when demand approaches or exceeds capacity.

## 2. Baseline Capacity

Lakeside General Hospital has **24 staffed adult ICU beds** across two units: MICU (12 beds) and SICU (12 beds). Either unit may accept patients of the other service when beds are tight ("boarding").

## 3. Capacity Status Levels

| Status | Occupancy | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Fewer than 20 beds occupied | Normal operations |
| Yellow | 20–22 beds occupied | Daily 08:00 and 16:00 bed huddles; expedite ICU transfers-out that meet criteria |
| Orange | 23–24 beds occupied | Bed huddles every 4 hours; open PACU overflow (up to 4 beds); notify Administrator on Call |
| Red | All beds full and PACU overflow in use | Activate Surge Plan; consider diversion of inbound transfers; CMO notified |

## 4. Bed Huddle

The bed huddle is led by the **House Supervisor** with the ICU charge nurses, the ICU attending, and the ED charge nurse. The huddle reviews every pending ICU admission and every patient who meets transfer-out criteria.

## 5. PACU Overflow

PACU overflow beds are staffed by ICU nurses at a ratio of **1:2**. The ICU team, not anesthesia, is responsible for patients in PACU overflow beds. Maximum PACU overflow stay is **24 hours**; longer stays require CMO approval.

## 6. Diversion

Only the **Chief Medical Officer (or designee)** can place the hospital on ICU diversion for inbound transfers. Diversion does not apply to patients arriving through the ED, who must still receive a medical screening examination and stabilization.

## 7. Triage When Demand Exceeds Supply

When there are more Priority 1 patients than available ICU or overflow beds, the ICU attending uses the priority framework in LGH-ICU-101 and consults the on-call **Triage Officer**. Triage decisions are documented and reviewed by the Critical Care Committee within 72 hours.
