# ICU Discharge and Step-Down Transfer Policy

**Policy number:** LGH-ICU-102 (synthetic)
**Owner:** Department of Critical Care Medicine
**Last reviewed:** January 2026

## 1. Purpose

To ensure patients are transferred out of the ICU safely, at the right level of care, and with a complete handoff.

## 2. Readiness Criteria for Transfer Out of the ICU

A patient may be considered ready for transfer to the Progressive Care Unit (PCU, step-down) or a general ward when all of the following are met:

- Off all vasopressors for at least **12 hours**
- Extubated for at least **24 hours**, or stable on a tracheostomy collar for 48 hours
- Oxygen requirement of 6 L/min nasal cannula or less
- No titratable infusions other than insulin per protocol
- Neurologic status stable for 24 hours
- No anticipated need for ICU-level intervention in the next 24 hours

## 3. Choosing the Destination Unit

- **Progressive Care Unit (PCU):** patients who still need continuous cardiac monitoring, vital signs every 2 hours, or a nurse-to-patient ratio of 1:3.
- **General ward:** patients who need vital signs every 4 hours or less often and a nurse-to-patient ratio of 1:5.

## 4. Handoff Requirements

- Physician-to-physician handoff must use the **I-PASS** format and occur verbally (phone or in person) before the patient leaves the ICU.
- Nurse-to-nurse handoff must be completed at the bedside or by phone, with the receiving nurse given the opportunity to ask questions.
- The ICU team writes a **transfer summary note** that includes active problems, lines and drains, code status, and pending results.

## 5. Night-Time Transfers

Transfers out of the ICU between **22:00 and 07:00** are discouraged because of higher readmission risk. Night transfers are permitted only when an ICU bed is needed for a Priority 1 or Priority 2 patient, and require approval from the ICU attending.

## 6. ICU Follow-Up After Transfer

The ICU outreach nurse reviews every transferred patient within **12 hours** of transfer and again at 24 hours. Any patient who meets Rapid Response criteria within 48 hours of ICU discharge should be flagged as an "ICU bounce-back" and reported to the Critical Care Quality Committee.

## 7. Readmission Metric

The ICU target is a 48-hour readmission rate below **4%**, reviewed monthly.
