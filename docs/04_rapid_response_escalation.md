# Rapid Response Team and Escalation of Care

**Policy number:** LGH-ICU-104 (synthetic)
**Owner:** Patient Safety Committee
**Last reviewed:** February 2026

## 1. Purpose

Describes when and how staff activate the Rapid Response Team (RRT) for deteriorating adult inpatients outside the ICU.

## 2. RRT Composition

The RRT consists of an **ICU nurse**, a **respiratory therapist**, and the **ICU fellow or night intensivist**. A pharmacist responds during daytime hours (07:00–19:00).

## 3. Activation Criteria

Any staff member, patient, or family member may activate the RRT. Activation is expected when any of the following is present:

- Heart rate below 40 or above 130 beats/min
- Systolic blood pressure below 90 mmHg
- Respiratory rate below 8 or above 28 breaths/min
- SpO2 below 90% despite supplemental oxygen
- Acute change in mental status
- Modified Early Warning Score (MEWS) of **5 or higher**
- Staff member is worried about the patient, even if no criterion is met

## 4. How to Activate

Dial the internal emergency extension **4-4444** (synthetic) and state "Rapid Response," the unit, and the room number. Family members can activate using the **Condition H** number posted in every room.

## 5. Response Expectations

- RRT arrives at the bedside within **10 minutes** of activation.
- The primary team's provider must come to the bedside or call the RRT within **15 minutes**.
- The RRT documents an assessment, interventions, and disposition: stays on the unit, transfers to PCU, or transfers to ICU.

## 6. Escalation to ICU

If the RRT determines the patient meets ICU criteria (see LGH-ICU-101), the RRT intensivist places the ICU consult and coordinates the bed with the House Supervisor. The patient remains under RRT care until the ICU team assumes responsibility.

## 7. Patients Who Stay on the Unit

Patients who remain on the ward after an RRT call receive vital signs every **1 hour for 4 hours**, then return to the ordered frequency if stable. A repeat RRT call within 12 hours triggers an automatic ICU attending review.
